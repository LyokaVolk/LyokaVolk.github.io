Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy covers this website and the Google connection used by authorized SHOWTAKE channel operators. The connection uses YouTube API Services. Visitors do not need to sign in.

Information and purpose

With the operator's permission, SHOWTAKE accesses channel identity, video and playlist details, comments and associated public author information, and channel analytics. It also processes operator-provided videos and metadata for approved uploads and channel updates. OAuth tokens maintain the authorized connection; SHOWTAKE does not request or store Google passwords.

Information is used to operate the authorized channel, manage approved uploads and comments, and review audience feedback and channel performance. Access depends on the permissions granted by the operator.

Use, sharing and storage

Access is limited to authorized operators and service providers needed for these functions, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security controls. Google processes requests made through its services. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Private channel information and credentials are not published on this website or in its public source repository.

Cached YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Connection credentials are kept only while needed for authorized access. Operator-created content is separate from temporary API caches.

SHOWTAKE's handling of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can revoke access through Google Account permissions. To request deletion of retained Google or YouTube data, or ask a privacy question, email showtake.tv@gmail.com. Verified deletion requests are handled within seven days. Removing the connection does not itself delete videos already stored on YouTube.

This website

This is a static information site with no sign-in, forms, advertising or added analytics. GitHub Pages hosts it and may collect technical request information, including IP addresses, for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google's services are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Updates

Changes are published here with a revised effective date. A new purpose or permission requires appropriate notice and consent before use.
